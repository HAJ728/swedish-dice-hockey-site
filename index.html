Swedish Dice Hockey

The notepad hockey game, as an app.

One round per page of the pad, the results thrown with a twelve-sided die, the table written underneath. You throw the dice yourself, the app keeps the league: standings, scorers, goaltenders, playoffs, ageing squads, promotion and relegation, and an archive of every season the book has lived through. Everything is saved on your own device.
